STEP 1 — AMADEUS (Hotel rates)  ~3 minutes
───────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to: https://developers.amadeus.com
2. Click "Register" → create free account
3. Dashboard → My Apps → Create New App
4. Copy "API Key" → paste as AMADEUS_CLIENT_ID in .env
5. Copy "API Secret" → paste as AMADEUS_CLIENT_SECRET in .env
6. Sandbox works immediately (test data).
   Apply for Production when ready (1–2 day review).
